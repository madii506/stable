How it works

Degen on the chart. Boomer in the payout.

Four things happen, and none of them need you after launch.

The coin launches paired with the index

On StonkFun, against SPYx (S&P 500) or QQQx (Nasdaq 100). The pair can't change.

Every trade leaves a small tax

Reward coins carry a transfer tax on every buy and sell. It goes to holders, not to the dev.

The tax is paid in the index

Because the coin trades against the index, the payout is the index itself.

Holders collect it, pro rata

The more you hold, the bigger your share. The meme does what memes do. The payout is the S&P.

FAQ

Questions.

What is STABLE?

Memecoins paired with the index. Every STABLE coin launches on StonkFun against the S&P 500 (SPYx) or the Nasdaq 100 (QQQx) as a reward coin, so the tax on every trade is paid to holders in the index.

Is the coin itself stable?

No. It's a memecoin and moves like one. The only stable part is what it pays you: the index.

Where do the numbers come from?

Coin counts, market caps and volume are read live from StonkFun's public API. Index prices come from Jupiter.

Is STABLE part of StonkFun?

No. STABLE is independent and built on StonkFun.
